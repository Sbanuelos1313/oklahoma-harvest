Delete Your From Our Place Account

Account and associated data deletion information

Request Account Deletion

From Our Place, operated by Chronos AI, allows customers and producers to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Contact From Our Place support using the support contact provided in the app or on our support page.
  2. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request.
  3. Provide the email address associated with your From Our Place account.
  4. State whether the account is a Customer or Producer account.

We may need to verify that you are the account owner before completing the deletion request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After a verified deletion request is processed, data associated with the account may include:

  • Account profile information
  • Name and contact information associated with the account
  • Saved account preferences
  • Producer storefront and product information associated with the account
  • User-submitted content associated with the account, where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when necessary to comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements, maintain security, or preserve required business and transaction records. This may include limited order, payment, accounting, tax, or transaction records for the period required by applicable law or legitimate business obligations.

Payment card information is processed by third-party payment providers and is not stored as full payment card data by From Our Place.
